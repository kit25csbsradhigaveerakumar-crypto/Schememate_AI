# 🧭 SchemeMate

### Multilingual Voice-First AI Assistant for Schemes, Exams & Public Services

> **"Speak naturally. Discover what fits you. Know what to do next."**

---

## 📌 Overview

**SchemeMate** is a multilingual, voice-first AI assistant designed to help users discover and access relevant **government schemes, scholarships, educational benefits, examinations, employment opportunities, and public services**.

Instead of requiring users to know the exact name of a scheme or service, SchemeMate allows them to simply **describe their need in their own language using voice or text**.

The AI understands the user's intent, asks relevant questions, identifies suitable opportunities, explains eligibility and required documents, guides the user through the application process, and helps them track their application.

---

## 🚨 Problem Statement

Many students and citizens face difficulties when trying to access government schemes, scholarships, examinations and public services because:

- Information is spread across multiple websites and portals.
- Users may not know the exact name of the scheme or service.
- Eligibility conditions can be difficult to understand.
- Application procedures can be complicated for first-time users.
- Required documents are often unclear.
- Regional-language users may struggle with English-only information.
- Users may not know how to track an application after submission.

### The real problem:

> **People often know what they need, but they don't know where to start or what they are eligible for.**

---

## 💡 Our Solution

SchemeMate provides a **single conversational AI interface** where users can simply say what they need.

For example:

> "எனக்கு படிப்புக்கு ஏதாவது financial support வேணும்."

Instead of asking the user to search for a specific scholarship name, SchemeMate:

1. Understands the user's request.
2. Identifies the user's intent.
3. Asks only the necessary questions.
4. Understands the user's eligibility.
5. Finds relevant opportunities from verified information.
6. Explains why an opportunity may be suitable.
7. Lists required documents.
8. Provides step-by-step application guidance.
9. Helps users track application progress and deadlines.

---

## 🎯 Key Features

### 🎙️ Voice-First Interaction
Users can communicate with SchemeMate using natural voice instead of typing complicated queries.

### 🌐 Multilingual Support
Users can interact in their preferred language.

The system is designed to understand:

- Tamil
- Hindi
- Kannada
- Telugu
- Malayalam
- English
- Other supported Indian languages

The goal is not just translation.

> **SchemeMate understands how people naturally communicate in their own language.**

### 🧠 Intent Understanding

Users don't need to know the exact scheme, scholarship or examination name.

For example:

> "I need financial support for my education."

The AI understands the underlying intent as:

`EDUCATION_SUPPORT`

and continues the conversation accordingly.

### 🎯 Personalized Guidance

SchemeMate asks relevant questions such as:

- Course
- Education level
- Year of study
- Location
- Other eligibility-related details

It then provides guidance based on the user's situation.

### 📋 Eligibility Guidance

The AI explains:

- Who can apply
- Eligibility requirements
- Important conditions
- Relevant limitations

### 📄 Document Guidance

The system explains which documents may be required, such as:

- Identity proof
- Educational certificates
- Income certificate
- Bank details
- Other scheme-specific documents
